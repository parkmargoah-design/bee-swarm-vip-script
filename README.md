## ✨ What is diple?

diple is a small, elegant application for your Mac that sits right in the notch at the top of your screen. It connects to your GitHub account and quietly keeps an eye on all your pull requests. When someone mentions you, requests your review, or updates a PR you care about, diple shows you right there in the notch.

No more switching tabs, checking emails, or missing important updates. Just look up at your screen and see what needs your attention.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is diple safe to use?

Yes! diple only reads your pull request information. It never changes code, deletes anything, or makes any modifications to your GitHub account. You can revoke access at any time from your GitHub settings.

### Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. diple is designed for everyone who uses GitHub, regardless of technical skill. If you can download and install an app, you can use diple.

### Will diple slow down my computer?

No. diple is extremely lightweight and uses minimal system resources. You won't even notice it's running.

### What happens if I close diple?

Your pull requests will still be waiting for you when you reopen it. diple doesn't change anything about your actual pull requests - it just shows you information.

### How do I update diple?

diple checks for updates automatically. When a new version is available, you'll see a small notification. Just click to update, and you'll have the latest features.
